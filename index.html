3-Minute Presentation Demo Script

  1. The Problem Statement (20s): "Manganese mine planning faces production shortfalls caused by weather and equipment constraints. We built MinePulse to connect spatial context with predictive shortfall planning."
  2. Geospatial Map Context (35s): "We select Dongri Buzurg mine in Bhandara, Maharashtra. Here we see public GSI manganese occurrence points, the IBM Yearbook official reserve report (14.58M tonnes), and the Sentinel-2 cloud-free surface context layer."
  3. Data Transparency Disclosures (15s): "Public geological and weather layers are real. Mine-level daily operations logs are proprietary, so operational rows are synthetically generated and explicitly labeled SIMULATED."
  4. Shortfall Machine Learning Forecast (45s): "Comparing our target vs. actual history, the Random Forest model achieves a holdout MAE of 25.89 tonnes versus 36.16 tonnes for the trailing moving-average baseline."
  5. What-If Action Scenario (40s): "By adjusting equipment downtime or blasting delays on the slider controls, the app dynamically re-estimates shortfall recovery in real-time."
  6. Limitations & Roadmap (25s): "Optical satellite data provides surface context, not subsurface ore tonnage. Validating this for MOIL deployment requires approved drill-hole and assay ground truth."
